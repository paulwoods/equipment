How dark should the dark mode background be?

This sets the overall mood — from near-black "void" to a softer charcoal.

VOID · #09090b

Near-Black (Zinc-950)

Maximum contrast. High drama. Used by Linear, Vercel, Figma. Feels premium and focused. The fuchsia accent will pop hard against this.

SLATE · #0f172a

Deep Slate (Slate-900)

Slightly blue-tinted dark. Feels technical and cool. Common in developer tools and dashboards. Gives depth without full void.

INDIGO-DARK · #1a1a2e

Deep Indigo-tinted

Custom dark with a subtle purple/indigo undertone. Complements fuchsia beautifully — creates a cohesive cool-to-warm story. Distinctive.

GRAPHITE · #18181b

Graphite (Zinc-900)

Neutral dark without blue tint. Warmer than slate. Clean, versatile. Good middle ground between void and charcoal.
