What shade of pink feels right for the accent?

This will be used for primary buttons, active states, links, and highlights throughout the app.

ROSE · #f43f5e

Rose Pink

Warm, energetic. Bold contrast on dark backgrounds. Feels modern and assertive — a classic "corporate pink."

FUCHSIA · #d946ef

Fuchsia

Electric, vivid. Strong personality. Makes a statement — more avant-garde, less conventional.

PINK · #ec4899

Hot Pink

The Tailwind pink-500 default. Balanced — neither too warm nor too purple. Versatile, readable, approachable.

BLUSH · #fb7185

Blush / Rose-400

Softer, lighter. Works well as a tint/hover color. Can feel less authoritative at full strength but very clean as a highlight.
